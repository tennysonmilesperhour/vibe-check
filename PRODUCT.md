# Vibe Check

Vibe Check is a private evening mood journal for people who want a quick ritual and useful patterns without turning reflection into another task list.

## Launch promise

In about one minute, a user records how the day felt. Vibe Check keeps the entry, reflects it back immediately, and reveals patterns after several days. Cosmic systems and tarot are optional reflection lenses, not factual or medical guidance.

## Launch loop

1. A device reminder opens the evening check-in.
2. The user records mood, energy, sleep, feelings, activities, and optional notes.
3. The app confirms the save and shows a simple reflection.
4. Patterns become useful after repeated entries.
5. A weekly review gives the user a reason to return.

## Launch scope

- Today check-in with device-local draft recovery
- Mood, energy, sleep, emotion, and activity patterns
- Personal people notes without cross-account linking
- Deterministic Loom calculations from verified profile inputs
- Tarot and oracle reflection
- Optional, rate-limited AI interpretation with clear disclosure
- Export, sign out, password recovery, and account deletion
- Native iOS reminders, sharing, network state, and haptic save feedback

## Not in version 1.0

- Cross-account profile linking or invitations
- AI-estimated birth charts or current planetary transits
- Objective “healing progress” scores
- Medical or diagnostic claims

## Product register

Authenticated app UI. The visual system is Golden Hour: deep sunset ritual surfaces, quiet near-white working surfaces, warm rose-brown ink, and restrained gold accents.
