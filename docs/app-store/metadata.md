## Description

Vibe Check is a private evening ritual for keeping the day without writing a long journal entry.

Record your mood, energy, sleep, feelings, activities, and the moments that mattered. As your history grows, Vibe Check helps you notice patterns across your real days.

Optional cosmic and tarot tools offer prompts for reflection. They are designed for personal exploration and entertainment, not medical advice or factual prediction.

Features:

- A guided one-minute evening check-in
- Draft recovery if you stop halfway through
- Mood, energy, sleep, activity, and relationship patterns
- Private device-scheduled reminders
- Tarot and oracle reflection
- Optional AI interpretations with clear limits
- Encrypted or plain data export
- In-app account deletion

Vibe Check does not sell personal data or use it for advertising.
