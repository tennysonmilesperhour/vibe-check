# App Store screenshot plan

Capture six portrait screenshots from the release build on an accepted 6.9-inch iPhone simulator or device. Use one consistent accepted size, no transparency, real app UI, a seeded demo account, and no private user information.

| Order | Screen | Caption |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Today before check-in | Keep the day in one minute |
| 2 | Mood/energy/sleep ceremony | Notice how today actually felt |
| 3 | Completed Today summary | A small ritual you can sustain |
| 4 | Patterns with at least seven entries | See what your days keep telling you |
| 5 | People pattern detail | Understand the relationships shaping your week |
| 6 | Loom or tarot result | Add reflection when you want it |

Use the strongest core-journal screens first. Cosmos and tarot should appear late so the listing accurately communicates that they are optional reflection tools, not the product's primary promise.

Before capture:

- Seed at least seven varied check-ins and two people.
- Remove debug notices, placeholder support text, and test email addresses.
- Keep all captions outside the device screenshot or use a consistent branded frame without covering the interface.
- Check every caption at thumbnail size and avoid claims of diagnosis, prediction, or guaranteed outcomes.
