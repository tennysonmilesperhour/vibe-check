# Astrology in the voice of the plants

Direction recorded September 8, 2026. Applies to the live astrology profile, deep readings, integrated readings, relationship reflections, and daily/weekly/monthly/yearly wisdom. It extends [the plant voice](plant-voice.md) and the [product foundation](product-foundation.md).

## Editorial direction

The user named Susan Miller's *Planets and Possibilities* and Steven Forrest's *The Inner Sky* as influences. Vibe Check uses original language, with the plants as narrator. Adopt accessible explanations of planetary symbolism, practical possibilities in everyday life, psychological development, and personal choice. Do not imitate either author's distinctive prose or present the reading as authored or endorsed by them.

Public context reviewed:

- [Publisher description of Planets and Possibilities](https://books.google.com/books/about/Planets_and_Possibilities.html?id=e4WUElIwj9QC): signs, ruling planets, mythic context, and everyday possibilities.
- [Susan Miller: Learn Astrology](https://www.astrologyzone.com/learn-astrology/): an educational path through planets, elements, qualities, signs, and chart sectors.
- [Steven Forrest: Natal Astrology Courses](https://www.forrestastrology.com/pages/natal-astrology-courses-ap): choice and free will, the Sun/Moon/Ascendant as a starting point, then planets, signs, houses, and aspects. This curriculum lists *The Inner Sky* as its text.
- [Steven Forrest: Books](https://www.forrestastrology.com/pages/books): the author's bibliography, including *The Inner Sky*.
- [Steven Forrest: When Astrology Fails](https://www.forrestastrology.com/blogs/astrology/when-astrology-fails): the limits of choosing one predicted event from a symbolic configuration.

These are public descriptions and teaching material, not a full-book review, licensed adaptation, or ingestion of either book. No book passages are reproduced. Further-reading links appear in the app.

## The experience

1. Begin with what is known. Sun, Moon, and Rising introduce purpose, care, and how someone approaches situations. Unknown placements remain blank.
2. Optional depth adds Mercury through Pluto, the North Node, entered houses, and the five major aspects. Explain a planet as a function, a sign as a symbolic style, and a house as an area of life. Slow outer-planet signs are shared by generations.
3. Read placements together without declaring a fixed personality, destiny, compatibility score, or guaranteed event. Sign names alone cannot establish an aspect.
4. Offer an experiment and a question. Daily reflections support a check-in; weekly and monthly reflections direct the reader back to their full record; yearly reflections invite review of commitments. These are rotating natal reflections, not calculated transit forecasts or analyses of journal data.
5. Keep the lived record primary. No chart excuses mistreatment, assigns suffering as a spiritual task, or determines the user's identity from calmness or distress. Somatic recommendations in the free reports continue to come from recorded or selected experience, not astrology.

## Data and calculation limits

The existing Sun/decan algorithm uses calendar boundaries. It is approximate, especially at sign or decan boundaries. Exact natal placements require chart calculations not implemented here. A user-entered Sun can override the date estimate; an explicit unknown Sun stays unknown. Manual houses depend on birth time, place, and house system. Entered aspects are validated for known, distinct chart points and a supported aspect, but the app does not verify angles or orbs. No transits, returns, house positions, or aspects are inferred from a sign.

New profile fields live in the existing `cosmic_profile.astrology` JSON. No database migration or changes to original user records are required. Optional systems can be disabled without losing journal access. Existing historical saved readings remain historical records; newly composed readings use the updated content.
