# Vibe Check visual direction: Nature Sanctuary

Updated September 8, 2026 from the user's direction. This is the current visual guidance for Vibe Check and supersedes earlier Golden Hour and twilight palette guidance wherever they conflict. The shared visual system is implemented in the app. See [asset provenance and playback behavior](sanctuary-assets.md).

## Creative brief

Vibe Check should feel like an elegant, luxurious sanctuary in nature: lush greenery, warm natural materials, soft light, quiet depth, and carefully composed space. The experience should feel alive through cinematic stock video backgrounds, generated imagery and symbols, and fluid animation throughout the key moments of reflection.

The latest palette comes from the user’s woodland reference image: moss and olive, shadowed forest, lichen, aged paper, and weathered wood. This reference supersedes the earlier pale sage and cream treatment. Luxury should come through art direction, typography, texture, composition, and polished interactions.

The [plant voice direction](plant-voice.md) establishes the narrative: Nature connects the experience, the plants speak together as the central guide, and optional chakra companions offer a deeper layer of reflection. Carry that botanical presence through the imagery, symbols, motion, and copy.

The [product foundation](product-foundation.md) sets the priority: Free daily reflection, the orbit, complete relationship and habit patterns, and weekly and monthly reports with somatic practices matched to recurring stress patterns. Give these surfaces the full sanctuary treatment. Charts must make difficult periods, positive periods, and missing entries easy to distinguish. Practice cards should connect the recorded pattern, the user's desired response, short instructions, a skip option, and optional feedback. Show self-reported alignment separately from mood or calmness; avoid visual scores that declare how much someone is their “true self.” Optional paid systems add depth after the free journey is complete.

## Typography, materials, and composition

- Elegant serif headings with a clear, comfortable UI typeface. Use a considered hierarchy, generous spacing, and restrained line lengths.
- Layer botanical textures, subtle paper grain, stone, and softly filtered light. Keep text and controls on stable, legible surfaces.
- Give imagery room to establish the atmosphere. Use gently rounded surfaces and fine gold details consistently.
- Carry the same direction through onboarding, navigation, check-ins, patterns, People, Practice, Cosmos, sharing, and empty states.

## Stock video backgrounds

Use cinematic nature footage: sun filtering through leaves, forest mist, a still woodland pool, slow water ripples, ferns moving in a breeze, and light across natural stone. Grade footage toward the green, cream, tan, and muted-gold palette.

Place video in the welcome experience, Today hero, and selected reflective moments. Favor a slow camera, seamless loops, and generous negative space. Add a stable forest overlay behind text, compose crops for phones as well as desktop, and keep navigation and controls immediately usable.

Background video should be muted, play inline, and have a coordinated poster image. Provide a visible pause control for persistent motion. Show the poster when reduced motion or data-saving preferences apply, playback is blocked, or loading fails. Pause offscreen and hidden-page playback. Record the source and usage rights for selected stock assets.

## Generated imagery and symbols

The approved app icon and logo are [B1 / Breath](breath-brand.md): a green
leaf formed from flowing wave filaments, paired with the lowercase
Instrument Serif wordmark. Use its shared vector source and generated assets
for all brand placements. Interface marks inherit the woodland palette, with
light variants on forest surfaces.

Create a cohesive family of botanical sanctuary scenes, reflective pools, moss and stone details, and subtle celestial imagery rooted in the natural setting. Use consistent lighting, grading, and composition across the collection.

Develop original botanical and celestial emblems with fine gold linework: leaves, seed forms, sun and moon motifs, organic rings, and geometry related to the Loom. Keep their stroke weight, visual density, and proportions consistent. Simplify approved motifs for small interface sizes so they remain recognizable.

Working image prompt: “An elegant luxury nature sanctuary, sage foliage and deep evergreen forest, warm cream stone and natural tan textures, subtle antique gold details, soft filtered sunlight, cinematic atmosphere, refined botanical composition, generous negative space for interface text, no lettering.”

Generated artwork supplies atmosphere and symbolic illustration. Actual charts and personal data visualizations retain their existing data-driven meaning.

## Animation and dynamics

- Use smooth fades, gentle reveals, subtle depth, and responsive hover, press, selection, and completion states.
- Start with 180–300 ms for controls and 350–650 ms for page and artwork transitions; tune for the interaction.
- Let check-in selections settle softly and confirmation appear immediately. Keep the next action available throughout transitions.
- Give the Loom a deliberate gold line reveal and quiet emphasis on a selected connection. Keep it easy to explore by touch and keyboard.
- Coordinate ambient video with interface motion so each scene feels calm. With reduced motion, use static artwork and immediate state changes.
