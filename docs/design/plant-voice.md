**Status: retired October 2, 2026.** The user retired the plants' collective voice. App copy now speaks plainly to the person, with no speaker label, no “we,” and no persona. The optional chakra plant companions stay as journal prompts and do not speak either. These rules from below still apply to them: the pairings are authored, symbolic, and carry no medical claim; give one line of origin context where a plant's symbolism has a specific origin; never encourage eating, smoking, burning, or harvesting a plant; and attribute any traditional teaching to the people who shared it. Everything else here, including the voice, speaking companions, and handoffs, is kept only as a record of the earlier direction.

## Narrative foundation

Vibe Check gives voice to and speaks for the plants. Nature connects people across cultures, times, and traditions. Plants provide the common thread through which the app introduces its systems and invites the user to understand their own life.

The plants speak together. No single plant is the teacher: the voice belongs to the living world as a whole, the way a forest is more than any one tree. The plants welcome the user, introduce each system, and help them carry what they discover into daily life. The experience gives plants a presence, a perspective, and a relationship with the user.

The plants' first responsibility is to help the user keep and understand their own record. Daily reflection, the orbit, complete pattern history, and weekly and monthly reports with relevant somatic practices form the free experience. Optional paid systems and plant journeys build on that foundation. A good day and the difficult days before it remain visible together.

The user's phrase “universal and unbiased common thread” sets a direction for hospitality: welcome people with different beliefs, give each tradition its own context, and leave room for the user to disagree with an interpretation. The app's authored voice still has a perspective; claims of objective neutrality would overstate what it can offer.

## Layers of the experience

1. **Nature is the shared ground.** The sanctuary, plants, light, water, and seasons establish where the experience takes place.
2. **The plants are the continuing guide.** One recognizable collective voice carries the user through check-ins, People, Patterns, Practice, and Cosmos.
3. **The user's lived record comes first.** The plants support free daily reflection, relationship and habit patterns, and weekly and monthly reviews with links back to what the user recorded.
4. **Each optional system retains its identity.** The plants introduce astrology, Human Design, Gene Keys, numerology, tarot, Enneagram, and chakras with clear explanations of their individual concepts and origins. Paid depth never gates the user's journal or charts.
5. **Each chakra can bring one plant forward.** The user may enter a more focused reflection with that plant, then return to the wider sanctuary.

The chakra layer is optional. Users can explore a system directly, and the app only draws connections between systems where it can explain the basis for doing so.

## The plants' voice

The plants speak with patience, warmth, directness, and curiosity. Their language grows out of concrete experiences: light, roots, thirst, weather, growing, resting, and returning. They listen closely to what the user has recorded and ask a useful question.

Use first-person plural (“we”) for authored plant dialogue. Keep factual observations legible beside that dialogue: the dates, scores, chart values, source traditions, and reasons a reflection appears should remain available. A symbolic interpretation should be recognizable as an invitation to reflect.

When a person records a difficult relationship, the plants help them revisit the whole history. They must not turn mistreatment into a spiritual lesson, imply a chart excuses harmful behavior, or urge reconciliation. Reports preserve the user's words and agency; a symbolic reading cannot overrule their account.

The plants also introduce the reports' body-based practices: explain which recorded stress pattern prompted the suggestion, connect it to the response the user wants to practice, and invite them to notice how it felt. For example: “You wrote about agreeing before knowing what you wanted in three entries. You said you want more time to decide. Does that connection fit? If you would like, we can practice a pause.” Follow the [somatic practice specifications](product-foundation.md#somatic-practice-recommendations). Plant imagery may accompany an invitation; it cannot establish a diagnosis, guarantee healing, or require a paid chakra journey to access the practice.

When guiding the movement from “not-self” to “self,” the plants use the person's descriptions of their values, needs, and chosen responses. Ask “Did you feel more able to choose?” and “Did that response feel like you?” Leave room for anger, sadness, and discomfort in an aligned choice. The plants do not declare someone's true identity or judge a protective response as failure. The [stress pattern guidance](product-foundation.md#recognizing-stress-patterns-and-returning-to-self) defines how reports support this reflection over time.

For immediate support with confusion, fight or flight, anger, shutdown, emotional numbness, or procrastination, the plants offer one concrete action before a longer reflection. For example: “You don't have to sort everything out at once. Let's find one small next step.” Follow the [actions for current feelings](product-foundation.md#actions-for-what-someone-is-feeling-now), accept the user's description, and ask what changed without promising relief. Plant metaphors must not delay instructions or imply a person has failed when a practice does not help.

The voice speaks for care of the living world as well as care of the user. Offer small opportunities for reciprocity, such as noticing a nearby plant, learning its name, tending a garden, or considering what a place needs. Progress in the app should never require acquiring or consuming a plant.

Labels and actions remain plain: “Save check-in,” “View history,” “Explore this chakra,” and “Return to the sanctuary.” The plants carry the narrative while the controls explain what will happen.

## Cultural attribution

Present the plants' voice as Vibe Check's creative framing. It does not represent any one tradition, teacher, or plant medicine. Attribute a traditional teaching to the particular people or teacher who shared it. Avoid describing a single belief as something held by all Indigenous peoples or presenting this combination of systems as an inherited teaching.

Give each chakra companion one line of context where its symbolism has a specific origin; for example, the lotus is central to Hindu and Buddhist iconography, and chakra texts describe every chakra as a lotus. Never encourage eating, smoking, burning, or harvesting a plant. Some companions (for example, mugwort) are allergens or unsafe in pregnancy, so the no-use rule is absolute.

Public descriptions can explain that the plant voices are authored reflective guides. A source record should keep the author or community, the specific teaching, its publication, and any permission relevant to reuse together. References establish context; they do not constitute endorsement of Vibe Check.

### Why the plants' voice was retired

On October 2, 2026 the user retired the collective voice as well. The notes it framed were short pieces of guidance, and presenting them as the plants speaking added a speaker and a personification that the text did not need. Notes now carry their content alone, addressed to the person in the second person.

### Why Tobacco was retired

Until September 29, 2026, Tobacco spoke as the “master teacher” and spokesperson for the plants. The user replaced it with the plants' collective voice after the expert review found three problems: tobacco is a sacred medicine for many Indigenous nations, and the “master teacher” framing echoed a specific Amazonian tradition while attributing a different one; commercial tobacco carries public-health associations the app should not appear to promote; and major ad platforms restrict tobacco content, which would put the brand's guide at odds with its marketing.

## Application to the existing sanctuary direction

Give the plants a recognizable botanical presence in the welcome experience, Today, and the transitions into each system. At the chakra layer, show the selected companion's name and artwork so the change of speaker is clear. Carry the sage, forest, cream, tan, and gold palette through these scenes.

Use living plant forms, leaves, flowers, seeds, and growing cycles in the imagery and motion. Keep the voice consistent across written copy and any future spoken narration. Develop the first complete sequence around welcome, one check-in, the orbit and pattern history, and the plants' weekly and monthly reflections. Extend that sequence with optional systems and chakra companions after the free journey is complete.
