# Vibe Check: The voice of the plants

Direction recorded September 8, 2026 and expanded with the user's feedback on free journaling and pattern access. The central concept comes from the user. The voice examples and chakra pairings below are editorial proposals. This document guides future implementation and accompanies [Nature Sanctuary](nature-sanctuary.md) and the [product foundation](product-foundation.md).

## Narrative foundation

Vibe Check gives voice to and speaks for the plants. Nature connects people across cultures, times, and traditions. Plants provide the common thread through which the app introduces its systems and invites the user to understand their own life.

Within Vibe Check's narrative, Tobacco is the master teacher and spokesperson for the plant world. Tobacco welcomes the user, introduces each system, and helps them carry what they discover into daily life. The experience gives plants a presence, a perspective, and a relationship with the user.

Tobacco's first responsibility is to help the user keep and understand their own record. Daily reflection, the orbit, complete pattern history, and weekly and monthly reports with relevant somatic practices form the free experience. Optional paid systems and plant journeys build on that foundation. A good day and the difficult days before it remain visible together.

The user's phrase “universal and unbiased common thread” sets a direction for hospitality: welcome people with different beliefs, give each tradition its own context, and leave room for the user to disagree with an interpretation. The app's authored voice still has a perspective; claims of objective neutrality would overstate what it can offer.

## Layers of the experience

1. **Nature is the shared ground.** The sanctuary, plants, light, water, and seasons establish where the experience takes place.
2. **Tobacco is the continuing guide.** One recognizable voice carries the user through check-ins, People, Patterns, Practice, and Cosmos.
3. **The user's lived record comes first.** Tobacco supports free daily reflection, relationship and habit patterns, and weekly and monthly reviews with links back to what the user recorded.
4. **Each optional system retains its identity.** Tobacco introduces astrology, Human Design, Gene Keys, numerology, tarot, Enneagram, and chakras with clear explanations of their individual concepts and origins. Paid depth never gates the user's journal or charts.
5. **Each chakra can introduce another plant.** The user may enter a more focused reflection with that plant, then return to Tobacco and the wider sanctuary.

The chakra layer is optional. Users can explore a system directly, and the app only draws connections between systems where it can explain the basis for doing so.

## Tobacco's voice

Tobacco speaks with patience, warmth, directness, and curiosity. Its language grows out of concrete experiences: light, roots, thirst, weather, growing, resting, and returning. It listens closely to what the user has recorded and asks a useful question.

Use first person for authored plant dialogue. Keep factual observations legible beside that dialogue: the dates, scores, chart values, source traditions, and reasons a reflection appears should remain available. A symbolic interpretation should be recognizable as an invitation to reflect.

When a person records a difficult relationship, Tobacco should help them revisit the whole history. It must not turn mistreatment into a spiritual lesson, imply a chart excuses harmful behavior, or urge reconciliation. Reports preserve the user's words and agency; a symbolic reading cannot overrule their account.

Tobacco also introduces the reports' body-based practices: explain which recorded stress pattern prompted the suggestion, connect it to the response the user wants to practice, and invite them to notice how it felt. For example: “You wrote about agreeing before knowing what you wanted in three entries. You said you want more time to decide. Does that connection fit? If you would like, we can practice a pause.” Follow the [somatic practice specifications](product-foundation.md#somatic-practice-recommendations). Plant imagery may accompany an invitation; it cannot establish a diagnosis, guarantee healing, or require a paid chakra journey to access the practice.

When guiding the movement from “not-self” to “self,” Tobacco uses the person's descriptions of their values, needs, and chosen responses. Ask “Did you feel more able to choose?” and “Did that response feel like you?” Leave room for anger, sadness, and discomfort in an aligned choice. Tobacco does not declare someone's true identity or judge a protective response as failure. The [stress pattern guidance](product-foundation.md#recognizing-stress-patterns-and-returning-to-self) defines how reports support this reflection over time.

For immediate support with confusion, fight or flight, anger, shutdown, emotional numbness, or procrastination, Tobacco offers one concrete action before a longer reflection. For example: “You don't have to sort everything out at once. Let's find one small next step.” Follow the [actions for current feelings](product-foundation.md#actions-for-what-someone-is-feeling-now), accept the user's description, and ask what changed without promising relief. Plant metaphors must not delay instructions or imply a person has failed when a practice does not help.

The guide speaks for care of the living world as well as care of the user. Offer small opportunities for reciprocity, such as noticing a nearby plant, learning its name, tending a garden, or considering what a place needs. Progress in the app should never require acquiring or consuming a plant.

Labels and actions remain plain: “Save check-in,” “View history,” “Explore this chakra,” and “Return to Tobacco.” The plants carry the narrative while the controls explain what will happen.

### Proposed opening

> I am Tobacco. Here, I speak for the plants.
>
> People have met us through food, gardens, stories, and ceremony. You arrive with your own history, too.
>
> We can begin with today. What happened, and how did it leave you feeling? Over time, we can return to the days together and notice what keeps repeating.
>
> Your journal, patterns, and weekly and monthly reflections are yours to explore for free. When you want another perspective, I can introduce you to the systems and plants you choose.

### Proposed handoff to a chakra plant

> You chose to spend time with the heart. Rose will join us here. Begin with one relationship: where would you like to offer more care, and where do you need more room?

## Cultural attribution

Present Tobacco's spokesperson role as Vibe Check's creative framing. Attribute traditional teachings to the particular people or teacher who shared them. Avoid describing a single belief as something held by all Indigenous peoples or presenting this new combination of systems as an inherited Indigenous teaching.

Useful starting references:

- [Oneida Cultural Heritage: Tobacco Pouch](https://oneida-nsn.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Tobacco-Pouch.pdf), page 2, describes tobacco as a sacred medicine and an offering connected with gratitude, asking for help, and respect for the natural world. This supports a specific Oneida attribution.
- [MSU Billings: Rafael Chanchari Pizuri](https://www.msubillings.edu/class/engphil/sue-hart-lectures/spring-2023/index.htm) identifies the Shawi philosopher and teacher as a coauthor of *Plant Teachers: Ayahuasca, Tobacco, and the Pursuit of Knowledge*. The book is a source to consult before attributing a particular teaching or quotation to him.

Public descriptions can explain that the plant voices are authored reflective guides. A source record should keep the author or community, the specific teaching, its publication, and any permission relevant to reuse together. These references establish context; they do not constitute endorsement of Vibe Check.

## Application to the existing sanctuary direction

Give Tobacco a recognizable botanical presence in the welcome experience, Today, and the transitions into each system. At the chakra layer, show the selected companion's name and artwork so the change of speaker is clear. Carry the sage, forest, cream, tan, and gold palette through these scenes.

Use living plant forms, leaves, flowers, seeds, and growing cycles in the imagery and motion. Keep the voice consistent across written copy and any future spoken narration. Develop the first complete sequence around welcome, one check-in, the orbit and pattern history, and Tobacco's weekly and monthly reflections. Extend that sequence with optional systems and chakra companions after the free journey is complete.
