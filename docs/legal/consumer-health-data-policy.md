## Consumer health data privacy policy

**Effective date:** [date of publication]

This policy explains how [legal entity name] ("Vibe Check", "we") collects,
uses, and shares consumer health data, and the rights you have over it. It
adds to the [privacy policy](/privacy), which covers all other information.

### What consumer health data is

Under Washington's My Health My Data Act, Nevada's SB 370, and similar laws,
consumer health data is personal information that is linked or reasonably
linkable to you and that identifies your past, present, or future physical or
mental health status. In Vibe Check this includes information you choose to
enter about your mood, emotions, stress, sleep, and energy, and your
responses to practices.

### Consumer health data we collect

Vibe Check stores only what you choose to enter. Depending on the features
you use, that can include:

- mood, energy, and sleep scores, stress levels, and the feelings you select;
- journal entries and notes;
- how interactions with people felt, including interactions you mark as
  strained or unsafe, and the notes you add about them;
- the stress states you choose (for example fight or flight, shutdown, or
  numbness), the practices you try, what changed afterward, and whether a
  response felt like you;
- a safety plan, if you write one;
- report reflections and saved drafts of any of the above.

We also keep your email address, optional name, preferences, and a record
that you confirmed you are 18 or older. These are not consumer health data on
their own, but they are linked to it.

### Where it comes from

All of it comes from you, through the app. The Help now pages work without
an account and save nothing. To suggest support services for your region, the
app reads your device's time zone and language on your device; that reading
is not sent to us. Vibe Check does not read HealthKit, your contacts, your
location, photos, or microphone, and does not buy or receive data about you
from anyone else.

### Why we collect and use it

Only to provide the service you asked for:

- to show your history, calendar, and the patterns in it;
- to prepare your weekly and monthly reports and suggest practices;
- to export your records when you ask, and delete them when you ask;
- to keep your account secure and prevent abuse.

Patterns and reports are calculated in the app. We do not send your consumer
health data to an AI service, use it for advertising, or use it to make
inferences for anyone other than you.

### Who we share it with

We do not sell consumer health data. We do not share it with advertisers,
data brokers, or other companies for their own purposes.

Service providers that store or deliver the app process it on our behalf and
under contract:

- **Supabase**, which stores your account and records and runs sign-in;
- **Vercel**, which hosts the app.

Vibe Check shares a sign-in service with two other apps from the same team,

We may disclose information if the law requires it. [Counsel: describe the

Nothing you write is shared with another person unless you choose to share

### Your rights

You can:

- **Confirm and access.** See everything Vibe Check holds about you by
  exporting it from Patterns or Settings.
- **Delete.** Delete your account and records from Settings. Deletion is
  permanent. [Counsel: state backup retention, if any.]
- **Withdraw consent.** Stop using a feature, delete entries, or delete your
  account at any time.
- **Correct.** Edit or delete entries in the app.
- **Ask for a list of service providers** who received your data.

If you can't use the app, email [support address] from the address on your
account. We respond within 45 days. If we need more time (up to 45 more days),
we will tell you why. We don't charge for requests, and we won't treat you
differently for making one.

### Appeals

If we decline a request, you can appeal by replying to our answer or emailing
[support address] with "Appeal" in the subject. We respond to appeals within
45 days and explain the outcome. If you are a Washington resident and your
appeal is denied, you can contact the Washington State Attorney General at
[link]. Nevada residents can contact the Nevada Attorney General at [link].

### Geofencing

Vibe Check does not use geofences around health care facilities or anywhere
else.

### Changes to this policy

If we change how we collect, use, or share consumer health data, we will
update this page and its effective date, and tell you in the app before the
change applies. We will ask for your consent first where the law requires it.

## Questions for the owner and counsel

1. **Entity and contact.** The legal entity name, a postal address, and a
   support address on the app's own domain (support currently goes to
   morphiclabsdata@gmail.com).
2. **Consent.** Whether the sign-up step (18+ confirmation and agreement to
   the terms and privacy policy) is enough, or whether the law needs a
   separate consent for collecting consumer health data. Collection here is
   limited to what's needed to provide the requested service.
3. **Cosmos fields.** Whether birth date and the optional symbolic profile
   count as consumer health data. They are kept out of the list above.
4. **Processors.** Data processing terms with Supabase and Vercel, and their
   regions.
5. **Retention.** Whether deleted records persist in provider backups, and
   for how long.
6. **The shared sign-in.** Whether Campground and Daily Digest count as
   affiliates under the Act, and whether the shared sign-in needs more
   disclosure.
7. **Other states.** Connecticut and others with consumer health data rules.
