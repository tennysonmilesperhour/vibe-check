# Vibe Check: Golden Hour

Vibe Check is a private evening mood journal designed to make consistent reflection practical. A guided check-in records mood, energy, sleep, feelings, activities, and optional notes in about one minute.

## How it works

1. A user completes a short evening check-in.
2. Vibe Check saves the entry and shows an immediate summary.
3. After repeated entries, Patterns shows changes across mood, energy, sleep, activities, and time spent with saved people.
4. A device reminder and Sunday review help users return without social pressure.

## Optional reflection tools

Tarot, oracle, astrology, numerology, Human Design, Gene Keys, enneagram, and chakra fields are optional. They are presented for reflection and entertainment. They are not medical advice, diagnosis, astronomical prediction, or a substitute for professional care.

AI interpretations are generated only when a user explicitly requests them. Vibe Check does not use AI to claim precise birth-chart calculations or current planetary transits.

## Privacy and control

App records are private to the user's account. Draft check-ins remain on the device. Users can export their history, optionally encrypt that export with a password, and permanently delete their account from Settings. Vibe Check does not sell personal information or use it for advertising.
